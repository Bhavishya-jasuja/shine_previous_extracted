Frequently Asked Questions

What is microscopic root canal treatment?

Microscopic root canal treatment uses advanced magnification technology to provide precise treatment of complex root canal cases. This ensures higher success rates and better outcomes.

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling us at 8791362345 or 9045452123, sending us a WhatsApp message at 8791362345, or using our contact form on this website.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your ID, any previous dental records, list of current medications, and any relevant X-rays or test results if available.

Do you offer single-visit root canal treatment?

Yes, Dr. Jatin specializes in single-visit root canal treatment using microscopic technology and rubber dam isolation for optimal results and patient comfort.

What are your clinic hours?

Our clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 7:30 PM. We are closed on Sundays.
